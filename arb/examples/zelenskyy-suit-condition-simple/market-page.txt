## Notes for arbitration framing

The arbitration proposition should not ask whether the market's `Final outcome: No` was right. It should ask the factual condition directly:

> Volodymyr Zelenskyy was photographed or videotaped wearing a suit between May 22 and June 30, 2025 ET.

The official Polymarket rule does not define `suit`. The packet therefore preserves both the ordinary-language evidence that many sources called the outfit a suit and the contrary argument that the outfit was a stylized military/formal jacket and trousers rather than a conventional suit.
