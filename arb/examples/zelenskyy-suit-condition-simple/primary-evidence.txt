# Primary evidence

## Direct proposition

Volodymyr Zelenskyy was photographed or videotaped wearing a suit between May 22 and June 30, 2025 ET.

## Official market condition

The official Polymarket rule text requires all of the following for a `Yes` condition:

1. Volodymyr Zelenskyy was photographed or videotaped.
2. He was wearing a suit.
3. The image or video was taken between May 22 and June 30, 2025 ET.
4. The image or video was released within that timeframe.
5. The image or video was authentic, not AI-generated or edited.
6. The resolution source was a consensus of credible reporting.

Exact Polymarket text:

> This market will resolve to "Yes" if Volodymyr Zelenskyy is is photographed or videotaped wearing a suit between May 22 and June 30, 2025 ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".
>
> For this market to resolve to "Yes" the images or video must be taken and released within this market's timeframe. The images or video must be authentic, not the result of artificial intelligence or video editing.
>
> The resolution source will be a consensus of credible reporting.
