This case tests the factual predicate behind Polymarket's “Clavicular pregnancy in 2026?” market without asking whether Polymarket or UMA resolved the market correctly.

Proposition: Between market creation on April 20, 2026, and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, Clavicular made a credible announcement that he and a partner were expecting a baby through pregnancy.

This is an open-record/search-enabled arbitration packet. The packet preserves the market rule, official Polymarket state, secondary reporting about the dispute, and known gaps. It does not attempt to exhaustively prove or disprove the pregnancy claim because the requested arbitration permits attorney web search.
