Relevant excerpt:

> QUESTION: So they haven’t told you anything about what’s going on?
>
> MR MILLER: They have been – we have been in conversations. They have been informing us about a number of operations. I know I’ve seen reports about ground operations. We’ve had some conversations with them about that. They have at this time told us that those are limited operations focused on Hizballah infrastructure near the border, but we’re in continuous conversations with them about it.

Additional excerpt:

> Israel has a right to defend itself against those attacks. That includes in targeting terrorist infrastructure inside Lebanon.

Record significance: the United States is a permanent member of the UN Security Council and the briefing occurred on September 30, 2024, within the market window. It is strong source-rule evidence that Israel had told the United States it was conducting limited operations focused on Hezbollah infrastructure near the border. It is weaker on the exact `intended to establish control` language.

## Israel / IDF statement record

Direct X page URL:
https://x.com/IDF/status/1840890054819864776

Saved direct X captures:

Direct X extraction from this environment produced only an error page, but Brave search and multiple captured news reports quote the IDF statement consistently:

> In accordance with the decision of the political echelon, a few hours ago, the IDF began limited, localized, and targeted ground raids based on precise intelligence against Hezbollah terrorist targets and infrastructure in southern Lebanon. These targets are located in villages close to the border and pose an immediate threat to Israeli communities in northern Israel.

Captured reports preserving this quotation include CBS, NBC, Al Jazeera, and BBC records listed in `secondary-reporting.txt` and `source-captures/`. Search-result snippets also identified the original X post.

Record significance: the quoted IDF statement is strong evidence that Israel announced ground raids in southern Lebanon. It characterizes the raids as limited, localized, targeted, and directed at Hezbollah targets and infrastructure in villages close to the border.

Relevant excerpt:

> Yesterday, the IDF notified UNIFIL of their intention to undertake limited ground incursions into Lebanon.
>
> Despite this dangerous development, peacekeepers remain in position. We are regularly adjusting our posture and activities, and we have contingency plans ready to activate if absolutely necessary.
>
> Any crossing into Lebanon is in violation of Lebanese sovereignty and territorial integrity, and a violation of resolution 1701. We urge all actors to step back from such escalatory acts, which will only lead to more violence and more bloodshed.

Record significance: UNIFIL confirms that on September 30 the IDF notified UNIFIL of an intention to undertake limited ground incursions into Lebanon. It does not by itself say that the crossing had already occurred before the September 30, 11:59 PM ET deadline.
