# Example 3a

This example is an open-record version of the Ronaldo mention dispute from [`examples/ex03`](../ex03).  The packet gives counsel the Kalshi market frame and the governing mention-market terms, then requires counsel to find evidence for the disputed mention and the event boundary.

It contains the proposition, the market-page note, and the full extracted text of Kalshi's `PRESMENTION.pdf`.  It omits the American Presidency Project reporter-exchange transcript and the Roll Call / Factba.se formal-draw archive note.  Counsel should search the public record, identify the transcript or other reliable evidence, and explain how the market rule treats remarks made at the venue before the formal program.

The dispute asks whether the strike `Ronaldo` counts for a market framed as `during the FIFA World Cup Draw` when the word was spoken in public remarks at the venue immediately before the formal program.  `ex03a` keeps that issue while forcing the lawyers to develop the evidentiary record themselves.
